# Research: what to add to make Moo Rush fun (2026-10-04, condensed)

> This is a condensed version of the report produced in the claude.ai chat (msg 33). The full text with all its sources is in that chat. ✅ = built in v9.

## Verdict
- **Don't add more systems yet.** Moo Rush already has more of them than My Mini Mart or Burger Please. What's missing is *feel*: a satisfying moment every couple of seconds, threats you can read at a glance, and funny big payoffs.
- **The 2-second rule** (from Azur Games' teardown of My Mini Mart): in the first loop, the player shouldn't spend more than about 2 s walking between actions. Five seconds or more turns the game into a "running simulator." **This conflicts with the v3 "one big world"**, and it's the core open tension.
- **Hay Day's designer's warning:** too many features make playing feel like an obligation. Cut or hide one system for every two you add.

## Diagnosis
1. Too much walking and too many things at once.
2. Threats punish without being funny. A gag needs a set-up and a punchline, like Untitled Goose Game.
3. No big highs: no Fever, no timed bosses, no secrets.
4. Growth you can't see. The world doesn't react to you becoming a tycoon or the President.

## Parked ideas, not built
- **Mini-games:** bull rodeo, fishing tension bar, milking rhythm, a pie-eating contest.
- **Collection:** a cow-breed album, farm decoration and trophies.
- **Story:** Grandpa Bovine as narrator, and Chad Cornwell, the rival who grows up with you. Newspaper cutscene cards.
- **Sharing:** share cards ("The Daily Moo" through the Web Share API), ghost visitors, local best scores.
- **Events:** weekly mutators (reuse the cheat system), calendar festivals.
- **A world that reacts to you:** merch, statues, paparazzi.
- **Signature gags:**
  - **Cow Cannon fast travel**, which also fixes the walking problem;
  - **the Tower of Babel stack** (no limit, it sways and can topple);
  - cured zombies becoming clumsy employees;
  - an inauguration parade followed by an alien "Galactic President" prestige;
  - a goose-mode night where you play the raccoon.

## Caveats
Some sources are fan wikis or consultancy teardowns. Keep randomness to fun surprises and avoid gambling-style pressure, since players will be of mixed ages.
